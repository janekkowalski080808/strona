Services in Denmark

Most frequently asked questions

Questions about projects in Denmark.

Answers before the form — without promises we cannot confirm.

Does Equss Outsourcing deliver projects in Denmark?

Yes. Denmark is one of the 37 countries from which we accept enquiries. They most often concern: factories, production plants and warehouses; farms and employers needing technical specialists. We confirm feasibility separately for each offer.

How long does it take to set up a team in Denmark?

To be determined after checking the employment route. We give an estimate after analysing the role, the team's citizenship and the required procedures.

Where do team members working in Denmark come from?

We source staff through partners in, among others: India, Nepal, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Philippines, Vietnam, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, Azerbaijan, as well as in other countries of Asia, Africa, the Americas and the Balkans. We match the country of origin to the regulations in force in Denmark and to the specific role.
