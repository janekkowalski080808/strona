In short

What is cleaning services at Equss?

In cleaning services, Equss takes over cleaning of a facility to an agreed standard and schedule, organises a supervised team and reports on performance — billing is usually per square metre, facility or period.

01

Multiple facilities

You want one contractor for several locations and one standard.

02

Shift work

The facility operates around the clock and needs staffing across different shifts.

03

Quality control

You need audits, checklists and a performance report.

04

Quick launch

A new facility or a change of contractor requires a smooth takeover.

Equss scope

What we take over in the process.

Client's role

What remains with the company.

  • access to the place of delivery and the necessary information;
  • quality requirements and the facility's safety rules;
  • identification of interfaces with the client's own organisation;
  • acceptance of the result against the agreed criteria;
  • no day-to-day direction of the contractor's team outside the agreed model;

Most frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions: cleaning services.

The answers come before the form so that the scope is clear before contact.

Do you provide materials and equipment?

We can, if agreed in the offer. The scope of materials and equipment is written into the contract.

How do you control quality?

Checklists, supervisor audits and a performance report. We set the acceptance criteria at the start.

Do you handle industrial cleaning?

Yes, after assessing the risks, health and safety requirements and qualifications needed at the given facility.
