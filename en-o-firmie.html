About us

B, C, C+E), hospitality, catering, cleaning, agriculture and construction in Poland and 36 other European countries.

What we do

Seven outsourcing models.

  • Production outsourcing — Taking over separate stages of production: assembly, packing, visual inspection, line operation.
  • Logistics and warehouse outsourcing — Order picking, parcel packing, receiving, loading and stocktaking under a results-based model.
  • Hospitality and catering — Housekeeping, kitchen support, dishwashing and back-of-house services — also seasonally.
  • Cleaning services — Cleaning of office, retail, logistics and industrial facilities.
  • Construction and technical works — Teams of welders, electricians, fitters and machine operators for separate scopes of work.
  • Drivers cat. B, C and C+E — Teams of van (B), truck (C) and combination (C+E) drivers with Code 95 (driver CPC) and a driver (tachograph) card.
  • International teams — Staff from Asia, Africa, the Americas and the Balkans — through a partner network, after verifying the legal route.

What we do not do

  • we are not a temporary employment agency and we do not assign workers to work under the client's direction;
  • we do not promise visas, permits or timelines without analysing the specific case.
Most frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about Equss.

Short answers on the operating model, reach and billing.

What does Equss Outsourcing do?

Equss Outsourcing is an outsourcing company. We take over agreed operational processes (e.g. order picking, packing, assembly, transport, housekeeping, facility cleaning, auxiliary construction work) and are responsible for their organisation and outcome. We are not a temporary employment agency and we do not assign workers to work under the client's direction.

How does outsourcing differ from temporary agency work?

In outsourcing, the contractor organises the work of its own team, supervises it and is responsible for the agreed result, and the client accepts the outcome against agreed criteria. In temporary agency work, the agency assigns workers to a user employer, who directs their work. Equss operates under the outsourcing model.

In which countries do you deliver projects?

We accept enquiries from companies in Poland and 36 other European countries, including Germany, Lithuania, the Czech Republic, the Netherlands, Austria, Spain and the Baltic states. We confirm feasibility separately for each project, after checking the role, the regulations and the employment route.

Where do team members come from?

We source staff through a network of partners in, among others, India, Nepal, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, the Philippines, Vietnam, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan and Azerbaijan, as well as in other countries of Asia, Africa, the Americas and the Balkans. We determine the country of origin separately for each project — it depends on citizenship, the role, the country of work and the applicable regulations.

How long does it take to set up a team?

There is no single number of days for all countries. The time depends on the country of work, citizenship, document readiness, required permits, accreditations and decisions of the authorities. We give an estimate only after analysing the specific project and do not treat it as a guarantee.

How is the service billed?

Billing reflects the scope and the method of acceptance: per unit of result (item, pallet, room, metre), per volume, per period or in a mixed variant. Financial terms and the contract are agreed individually.
