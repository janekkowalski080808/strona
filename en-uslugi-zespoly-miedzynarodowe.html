In short

What is international teams at Equss?

When the local market does not cover demand, Equss builds a team from staff sourced through a network of partners in, among others, India, Nepal, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, the Philippines, Vietnam, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan and Azerbaijan. We determine the country of origin separately for each project, in line with the regulations of the country of work.

Most frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions: international teams.

The answers come before the form so that the scope is clear before contact.

Do you guarantee a visa or permit?

No. Decisions belong to the competent authorities. We estimate the realistic time only after analysing the specific project.

How long does it take to bring in a team?

There is no single number. It depends on the country of work, citizenship, documents, permits, accreditations, the embassy and decisions of the authorities. Historical timelines (e.g. approx. 45–55 days for one route in North Macedonia) are always confirmed afresh.

Why are accreditations needed for the Philippines and Sri Lanka?

These countries require additional accreditations on the part of the entities involved in sending workers abroad. We check them before the project starts.
