Most frequently asked questions

Questions about countries of delivery.

The key rules before you send an enquiry for a specific country.

In how many countries does Equss Outsourcing operate?

We accept enquiries from 37 countries: Poland, the Baltic states, Central and Western Europe, the British Isles, Scandinavia, Southern Europe, the Balkans, as well as Moldova and Belarus. The list shows areas of activity, not ready orders — we verify each project separately.

What determines whether a project can be delivered in a given country?

The citizenship of the team, the role, the employer or principal, and the regulations of the country of delivery. In some countries, language requirements, recognition of qualifications or the seasonality of the offer must also be checked.

Do you give fixed timelines for each country?

No. For a few countries we know historical timelines (e.g. Lithuania, Austria, Serbia, North Macedonia), but we always confirm them afresh for each specific case. For other countries we set the timeline after analysis.
